# Multi-factor authentication (MFA)

Install an authenticator app on your phone, then open the security portal and choose "Add authenticator". Scan the QR code shown on screen and enter the six digit code to finish.

SMS text messages are not supported for MFA.

When you enroll, the portal gives you 10 one-time backup codes. Store them somewhere safe; each code works once.

If you lose your phone, contact the help desk. They will verify your identity with approval from your manager, then reset your MFA so you can enroll a new device.
