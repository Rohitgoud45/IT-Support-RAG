# Shared drives and file access

Team files live on SharePoint team sites. The old S: network drive is read-only and will be retired.

To get access to a team site or folder, open the site and click "Request access". The site owner gets the request and approves it. If no owner responds within 2 business days, open a ticket and the help desk will contact the department head.

Deleted files can be restored from the site's Recycle Bin for 93 days. After that, open a ticket; backups are kept for 1 year.

Do not share files with personal email addresses. External sharing is allowed only with approved partner domains.
