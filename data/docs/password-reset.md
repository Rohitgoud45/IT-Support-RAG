# Password reset and account lockout

You can reset your own password in the self-service portal. Sign in with MFA, choose "Reset password", and pick a new one.

Password rules: at least 14 characters, and you cannot reuse your last 10 passwords. Passwords expire every 90 days, and you get an email reminder 14 days before expiry.

After 5 failed sign-in attempts your account is locked for 30 minutes. The lock clears on its own. If you cannot wait, the help desk can unlock it after verifying your identity.
