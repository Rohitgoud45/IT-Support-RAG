# Email and Outlook problems

If Outlook is stuck on "Disconnected" or "Trying to connect", check that you can open webmail at mail.northwind.com. If webmail works, the problem is on your laptop: close Outlook, open Control Panel, Mail, Show Profiles, add a new profile, and set it as the default.

Your mailbox limit is 50 GB. When you reach 49 GB you get a warning, and at 50 GB you can no longer send email. Empty Deleted Items and move old mail to the Online Archive to free space.

To set an out-of-office reply, open File, Automatic Replies in Outlook, or Settings, Mail, Automatic replies in webmail.

Shared mailboxes such as helpdesk@ or payroll@ are added by the help desk after the mailbox owner approves your request in the service portal. They appear in Outlook within 2 hours.
