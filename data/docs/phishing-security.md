# Suspicious emails and security incidents

If an email looks suspicious, do not click links or open attachments. Use the "Report phishing" button in Outlook, which sends it to the security team and removes it from your inbox.

If you already clicked a link or entered your password, reset your password right away in the self-service portal and call the security hotline at extension 5555. The hotline is staffed 24 hours a day.

If your laptop is lost or stolen, call the security hotline within 1 hour so the device can be locked and wiped remotely.

IT will never ask for your password or MFA codes by email, chat, or phone.
