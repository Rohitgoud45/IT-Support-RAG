# Teams calls and meeting rooms

If people cannot hear you in Teams, click the three dots in the meeting, open Device settings, and check that the right microphone is selected. Then check that Windows has not muted it under Settings, System, Sound.

If your camera shows a black screen, close any other app that uses the camera, such as Zoom, and rejoin the meeting. Some laptops also have a physical privacy shutter or a camera key on the keyboard.

Meeting rooms have a Teams Room console on the table. Tap "Join" on the console to start a meeting booked in that room. To share your screen, tap "Share" on the console or plug the HDMI cable into your laptop.

If a meeting room console is frozen, hold the power button on the back of the console for 10 seconds to restart it.
