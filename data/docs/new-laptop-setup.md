# New laptop setup

New and replacement laptops are shipped ready to use. Sign in with your company email and password, then approve the MFA prompt on your phone. Setup takes about 30 minutes while apps install automatically; keep the laptop plugged in and connected to the internet.

Your files sync from OneDrive after you sign in. Files saved only on the old laptop's desktop or C: drive do not move automatically, so copy them to OneDrive before you hand the old laptop back.

Return the old laptop to the IT desk within 14 days. Remote employees get a prepaid return box.

Docking stations are on the approved hardware list in the service portal. Monitors and keyboards can be requested there too, with manager approval.
